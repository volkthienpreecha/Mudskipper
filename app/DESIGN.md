# Design context

Mudskipper is a field-analysis workbench for engineers inspecting rice-field radar observations. Use solid white panels on a quiet neutral application shell. The map is the primary content: a flat, naturally colored satellite view, restrained field fills, legible boundary outlines and one coral-red selected field. Keep purple and yellow as field-state colors. Controls sit above the map; the state key and time-scaled acquisition timeline sit beneath it. The field inspector is an adjacent solid panel, stacked after the map on small screens. Never obscure the data with glass cards, blur, ornamental gradients, floating chrome or a giant introductory hero. Dune Rise remains the wordmark and sparse page-title face; Asimovian is reserved for brief headings and explanatory statements. Controls use system sans, figures use tabular numerals, labels use sentence case, and captions stay at least 12px. Keep backend/model outputs and their run identity intact.
