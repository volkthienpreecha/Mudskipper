# Design context

Mudskipper is a field-level radar exploration and inspection-planning instrument. The product is precise, cinematic and tactile: agricultural satellite imagery sits beneath smoked glass controls. The map and selected-field telemetry carry the visual weight. Purple and yellow encode field states; red marks the selected field, active pass and missing-data band. Context imagery is attributed separately from radar overlays. Dune Rise is reserved for large page titles, Asimovian for sparse headings, and system sans and monospace for controls and data. Glass surfaces use an opaque fallback and exist to maintain legibility over imagery.
