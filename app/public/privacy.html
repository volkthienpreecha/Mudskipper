Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026 · hackathon draft

Short version: Mudskipper does not ask who you are, does not use cookies and does not track you.

What we collect

  • Nothing personal. There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Files you upload to rank for inspection. If you upload a GeoJSON of claims to POST /api/audit, the submitted claims are sent to the audit service, which returns a ranking and audit ID. Do not include farmer names, phone numbers or other personal details in uploads.
  • Server logs. Hosting and API providers may process request information, such as IP address and time, under their own policies.

Third parties your browser contacts

  • The site host and API service, which serve the application and data.

Each of these receives your IP address when your browser loads a file from it.

Field data

Field outlines and states come from satellite radar and public maps. They describe land, not people, and we do not link fields to owners.

Children

The site is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
