NASA and partner data

  • NISAR L2 GCOV (provisional, CRID P05023), NASA-ISRO, via the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC. Granule IDs are on the provenance page.
  • OPERA DSWx-S1 surface water, NASA JPL, used as a cross-check.
  • VIIRS active fires (VNP14IMG, VJ114IMG, VJ214IMG) via NASA FIRMS / Earthdata, used as a validation check.
